## What each note covers

1. Investment view and key figures (market cap, P/E, P/B, dividend)
2. Company background
3. The catalyst or turnaround driving the thesis
4. Financial performance, with tables and a chart
5. Management and ownership
6. Valuation scenarios (bear, base, constructive, bull)
7. Risk table: why each risk matters and what to monitor
8. Position and risk management
9. Conclusion, disclaimer and sources

---

## Method

- **Data:** annual and interim financial statements filed on the Nigerian Exchange, company results presentations, regulator announcements (NAICOM, CBN) and financial news reports.
- **Ratios:** I calculated price-to-earnings and price-to-book myself from reported figures and the share price on the date stated in each note.
- **Scenarios:** valuation scenarios apply different multiples to reported or guidance-implied earnings and book value. They show how sensitive the stock is to different outcomes. They are not price targets.
- **Assumptions:** where I assumed a number (for example, the FY2026 EPS used in the First HoldCo note), the note says so and explains how I derived it.
